# Cat Asset Options

These are free-to-use starting points for a Cat Gatekeeper-style break screen. Verify the license on the asset page again before shipping, because stock-site terms can change.

## Best Matches

1. Pixabay: Walking cat animation with green background  
   https://pixabay.com/videos/cat-animation-anime-cartoons-186488/  
   Why it fits: animated cat, greenbox/green background, easier to chroma-key into a screen takeover effect.  
   License shown: Pixabay Content License, free for use and download.

2. Pixabay: Cat transparent video search  
   https://pixabay.com/videos/search/cat%20transparent/  
   Why it fits: likely source for overlay-friendly clips. Look for simple motion, short duration, and minimal background clutter.  
   License shown by Pixabay search pages: royalty-free videos.

3. Pixabay: Cat animation video search  
   https://pixabay.com/videos/search/cat%20animation/  
   Why it fits: broad set of cartoon/animated cat videos for a playful forced-break feel.  
   License shown by Pixabay search pages: free download / royalty-free videos.

4. Pexels: A Staring Cat  
   https://www.pexels.com/video/a-staring-cat-8944050/  
   Why it fits: a direct staring cat can feel like the gatekeeper is judging the user during the countdown.  
   License shown: free to use.

5. Pexels: Cat Looking Around  
   https://www.pexels.com/video/cat-looking-around-9469828/  
   Why it fits: calm close-up cat footage for a softer break screen background or cut-in video.  
   License shown: free to use.

6. Pexels: Tabby Cat  
   https://www.pexels.com/video/tabby-cat-856344/  
   Why it fits: simple cat portrait footage that can be cropped into the break screen.
